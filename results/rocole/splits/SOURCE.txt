Project-side processing:
This project creates its own dataset split manifests, group identifiers,
prompt/target records and evaluation metadata.

Original image files are not redistributed in this repository.
The manifests reference relative image paths.

The OOD samples are project-generated synthetic samples,
not original RoCoLe images.
